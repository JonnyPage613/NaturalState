Customer communication · next

An arrival update without another phone call.

I would pair the assigned job with the truck’s trip, then send useful updates at these points:

  1. Driver starts the assigned trip

    Confirm the destination and departure. Send one on-the-way message with an arrival window.

  2. GPS refreshes the tracking page

    Show the vehicle, remaining road distance and last-update time. Send a new text for a material delay, rather than every GPS report.

  3. Truck approaches and arrives

    Optionally send a 15-minute notice. Confirm arrival and end that customer’s location-sharing session.

Implementation notes, accuracy and rollout costs

What this preview demonstrates

The map loads real USGS, Arkansas parcel and USDA soil services. The public park address anchors fictional work activity; pits, well and animated truck are demonstrations. The truck’s short path illustrates motion, not a calculated road route. Public data availability varies. A WebGL-capable browser and internet access are needed for the interactive map.

Field accuracy and filing

Published parcels are reference outlines, not surveyed boundaries. Soil units guide preparation; your field observations determine the site record. The 100-foot ring is a planning aid whose usefulness depends on the well’s recorded position and the applicable site requirements. You verify measurements, professional work and filing requirements. Submit through the official ADH portal and retain its generated output, receipt and decisions; this preview has no direct submission integration.

Shared storage and closeout

Current field demonstrations use visitor-session records and browser-local evidence storage. Protected shared files, complete official field mapping, permissions and tested recovery must be connected before this becomes the operational archive. The existing PHP/MySQL backend provides a starting point for intake, authentication and uploads. The production closeout check will validate the stored packet and required authority decisions.

Trips, notifications and privacy

The driver’s explicit Start trip action is the launch rule. Optional automation can match a preassigned trip with shop-geofence departure and sustained movement. GPS position plus road routing supplies arrival estimates. Initial rules to test: a 10-minute material delay and a stale-feed threshold suited to the provider. Send opted-in messages with delivery tracking, retries and duplicate suppression. An expiring customer link exposes only the active assigned trip. Stop sharing at visit end; retain last-update time and label phone fallback.

Devices, hosting and budget

Tracker figures above are USD planning allowances reviewed November 18, 2026, rather than purchase quotes. Confirm vehicle compatibility, carrier coverage and provider integration access before buying. Detached trailers and excavators need suitable asset devices. Existing shared hosting supports the map and PHP/MySQL app. Traccar runs on a suitable server, such as the planned VPS. Hardware, cellular, routing and SMS usage are separate provider expenses. Supabase Pro and MapTiler are optional, not launch requirements.

Optional expansion

Once the first truck and shared records work reliably, I can add vehicles, offline regional packages and useful AI summaries—daily visits, overdue callbacks and plain-English job lookup. Software-use rights, company records and professional work products belong in the separate business discussion.
