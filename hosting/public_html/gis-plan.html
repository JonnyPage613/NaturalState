What the system is

A GIS-enabled field service management application for soil evaluation and septic-design projects. It connects installer requests, property context, test-pit excavation, engineering observations, backfill visits, submission records and customer updates under one job reference.

The custom part: Jonny Page’s website, application code, role-specific handoffs, job history, document organization and integrations. The public maps and third-party trackers are not proprietary Natural State data. Company records, professional work products and software-use rights would be addressed in the parties’ business agreement.

How the truck becomes an active job trip

Recommended launch rule: dispatch assigns a job, vehicle and driver; the driver selects Start trip before driving. That identifies which customer is being visited. The vehicle feed then confirms movement and provides location updates. Driving north on Highway 65 alone does not identify a job.

Optional automatic mode: dispatch pre-assigns an accepted trip to that vehicle and time window. The system starts it after the tracker reports the vehicle leaving the shop geofence and sustained movement. A proposed initial setting is a 300-metre shop boundary and 60 seconds of movement; those are configurable starting values to test, not tracker defaults. If two trips could match, ask the driver or dispatcher instead of guessing.

There is no need to wait until the truck is five miles from the shop. Start the customer update once the intended trip and departure are confirmed. A changed assignment, cancellation or fuel stop stays attached to the active trip.

When the customer receives updates

TriggerCustomer experience
Assigned trip starts and departure is confirmedOne “Our team is on the way” message, an estimated arrival and a private tracking link.
Fresh vehicle coordinates arriveThe tracking page refreshes location, last-update time, remaining road distance and arrival estimate. It does not send a text for every GPS ping.
Estimate changes materiallyA new message if the arrival shifts by a configured amount—proposed starting point: 10 minutes—with duplicate suppression. Ordinary stops simply move the estimate.
Approaching the siteOne optional “about 15 minutes away” update, driven by travel time rather than a fixed mileage radius.
Location feed becomes staleDisplay “last updated” and stop presenting a stale point as current. Proposed initial stale threshold: two minutes, tuned to the provider’s update interval.
Arrival is confirmed / trip endsMark arrival and stop customer location sharing at the agreed end of the visit. A geofence can suggest arrival; the driver can confirm it.

The proposed delivery channel is opted-in SMS with a secure, expiring tracking link; email is a fallback. The link shows only that customer’s active assigned trip, not other jobs or off-duty vehicle movements. Message consent, provider registration, delivery status and retries are part of activation.

GPS supplies position; a road-routing service calculates remaining distance and arrival time. Routing should account for the truck, trailer and road restrictions where supported. Neither MapLibre nor a language model provides road travel times by itself. Customer notification rules can run without AI; AI can summarize a delay or assist dispatch later.

Phone plus vehicle tracker

Primary: an installed vehicle tracker reports while the truck is operating, independent of whether the driver has the webpage open. It still depends on power and cellular coverage.

Companion: the phone handles the assigned job, Start trip, access notes, photos and arrival confirmation. Phone GPS can be an explicitly enabled fallback while the app is active. Reliable background phone tracking would need an appropriate native app / supported location service; a normal browser page can be suspended.

Record the source, vehicle, driver, device time, received time and accuracy. Prefer the assigned vehicle’s fresh tracker report; label any switch to phone position. Do not average conflicting points or silently treat the driver’s location as the equipment’s. Store delayed reports as history rather than sending old “on the way” alerts after reconnection.

AI features worth considering later

Geotab Ace offers plain-language questions about fleet data. Samsara’s September 2026 update describes its assistant extending into the Driver App. These are current vendor capabilities to evaluate, not features already connected here.

For Natural State, useful first AI tasks would be summarizing a day’s visits, highlighting overdue callbacks or explaining a logged delay. Do not buy a larger fleet platform solely for an AI label. Job assignment, GPS, routing and reliable records deliver the core benefit.

Every job keeps its submission record

Prepare the information in the job file, then Tylor uses the official Arkansas permit portal to submit the required entries and attachments. Its instructions explain that the portal generates an EHP-19E. Retain that output, the exact application entries, drawings, supporting specifications, receipt / confirmation and later authority decisions.

Each revision becomes a new packet version. Closeout requires the completed fieldwork, required approvals and the retained submission packet plus final decision. The browser prototype checks retained files before offering closeout and records the packet manifest; production must enforce that against protected shared files on the server, with backups and tested recovery.

There is no verified direct ADH submission API connected here. A stored draft alone is not a portal receipt. The current archive is a browser-local prototype, not the final shared backup. The complete official field mapping and shared storage must be verified before real customer use.

On-site surveying and engineering records

Select a job to move from dispatch into its on-site map. Base-map choices and independent overlays stay in the bottom dock. Record typed points, lateral lines, primary / reserve outlines and map measurements; export the retained features as GeoJSON.

Water-well pins generate 100-foot geodesic planning buffers. The ring does not establish the accuracy of the well pin or replace measured separation checks. Every feature retains a source, accuracy when known, observation notes and engineering verification status. Phone / external-receiver integration depends on the device; no Bluetooth receiver has been connected in this preview.

The engineering record separates elevation shots in the entered project datum from primary / secondary soil observations in inches, loading rates, system configuration, treatment / disposal choices and health-authority references. The construction-approval anniversary uses the item 21 date, with a revalidation reminder if construction has not started. Saving this record neither signs EHP-19 nor issues a permit.

On-way, delay, arrival, photo, evaluation and invoice events create job-linked preview notification hooks. HTTPS Pay Now links are separate from cash / check payment records. Actual messaging, GPS and payment processing remain disconnected.

Build sequence and effort

  1. Already in the working preview: public map layers, job-linked handoffs and dates, team notes, cost / mileage examples, truck / ETA mock-up and local packet archive with a closeout check.
  2. Next: shared job records and archive — moderate work. Map all required application information, store files outside public access, keep versions and permissions, and test backups and restoration.
  3. First customer updates — moderate integration. Connect an opted-in message service and secure customer page. Start with driver-confirmed departure and an entered ETA if no tracker is installed.
  4. Live tracking — more involved. Trial one device, connect its feed and road routing, match trips, test dead zones and stale data, and tune notification rules before enabling customer tracking.
  5. Scale and offline use later. Add vehicles, asset trackers, regional offline map packages and optional AI reporting after the single-truck workflow is dependable.

The map display can stay on the existing PHP/MySQL hosting. Incoming vehicle updates and light processing can begin there; continuous polling or larger background workloads may justify the planned VPS. The map sources do not require Supabase Pro or MapTiler. Paid hardware, cellular, routing and messaging choices are explicit later decisions.
