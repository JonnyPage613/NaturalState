01 · Customer access

A professional website with a clear next step

Website & intake interface built

Find the right service and request a visit

Customers can identify what they need, enter an address and county, describe their project, and provide access notes and supporting files. Builders and installers have a repeat-request path.

The finished booking connection will offer approved availability and create a confirmed appointment only after the reservation is saved. Customers can also request an evening callback.

Voice connection in launch scope

24/7 AI receptionist

Give callers an answer while you’re on the excavator or evaluating a soil pit. I’ll configure the 24/7 AI receptionist to explain your services, collect property details, book approved available slots and confirm the saved appointment. Technical questions go into your callback queue with the details already recorded.

This provides around-the-clock call coverage and intake support. Live voice minutes and telephone service are separate operating costs.

02 · Field coordination

One job record from request through closeout

The installer, operator and engineer work from the same assigned job. Each handoff records who acted and when, with the information needed by the next person.

1 · Request

Property, service, access, plans and requested timing.

2 · Prepare the site

Assigned visit, pit locations, photographs and field notes.

3 · Engineering

Soil and elevation records, design details, permit references and changes.

4 · Complete the record

Backfill, invoice, payment status and retained submission packet.

03 · GIS & engineering records

Dispatch mapping and on-site tools in the same app

Map tools & forms built

Record the features that affect the job

Switch from the dispatch map to an on-site view. Record soil pits, wells, structures and stub-outs, tanks, distribution boxes, benchmarks and lateral lines. Draw primary and reserve areas and display true-scale 100-foot reference circles around well pins.

Public parcel, soil and terrain layers provide context. Field measurements, their source and accuracy stay attached to the record for engineering verification.

Shared archive in production release

Keep what was actually submitted

Retain photographs, measured elevations, primary and secondary soil criteria, drawings, application entries and the exact attachments sent to the state. Store submission confirmations and later decisions as dated versions.

Production closeout will check the required server-held records. The engineer continues to submit through the official permit portal; the internal archive keeps the supporting evidence.

04 · Vehicle & customer updates

Tell customers when their assigned crew is on the way

Connect a truck tracker or supported phone feed to the assigned visit. A driver-confirmed departure is the initial trigger; assigned-job geofences can automate it after testing. The system will send an arrival estimate and record delays, arrival and completion updates.

Customer messages disclose the visit status and estimated arrival window. A private tracking link can be enabled for the assigned customer during that visit. The public website will not display the fleet.

Trip records will connect distance, dates, vehicle and business purpose to a job number. Drivers can review missed trips and distinguish personal travel before export.

05 · Data protection & payments

Private business records, controlled staff access

I’ll set up encrypted connections, individual staff logins and separate access to Engineering and Siteworks records. Each person sees the jobs and documents assigned to their role; shared work is deliberately assigned.

I’ll configure automatic database and document backups, keep a separate recovery copy and test restoration. Customer-facing pages stay separate from private staff records.

Stripe-hosted invoices and payment links are the proposed payment connection. Customers will pay by supported card or bank methods through the provider; the website will retain the invoice reference and status. Cash and check payments can be recorded against the same job. Payment-processing fees are paid separately.

06 · Job costs & bookkeeping

Keep the records your bookkeeper needs throughout the year

Connect invoices, received payments, labor, equipment use, expenses and reviewed business mileage to their job numbers. Export CSV ledgers and a printable PDF summary for the bookkeeper or accountant.

Accounting-specific export formats for QuickBooks Online or Xero can be added against their supported import templates. Direct accounting synchronization and tax-return preparation are separate integrations; the platform’s launch purpose is to produce organized, reviewable records.

07 · Advertising & local visibility

Build the ads, launch the campaigns and track the work they bring in

My monthly service includes advertising setup, ad copy and graphics, an agreed local campaign plan, and ongoing performance review. Engineering and Siteworks pay for their own advertising. Campaigns use company-owned accounts with an approved spending limit.

Online advertising and business listings

  • Google Search: reach people actively looking for soil evaluations, septic design or site services.
  • Facebook and Instagram: create local ads with clear service offers, useful images and a direct booking or inquiry link.
  • Microsoft Advertising / Bing and Nextdoor: evaluate additional local channels after the initial campaigns show which inquiries become work.
  • Local listings: help set up and maintain accurate Google Business Profile, Bing and Apple business information where appropriate.

I’ll match each campaign to your approved service area, with county-specific ads for Boone, Benton, Washington, Carroll and Madison where you choose to work. Each ad leads to the relevant company’s service page and request form. Eligible Siteworks listings can also highlight verified veteran-owned credentials.

Local advertising and sales materials

Prepare service flyers, builder and realtor information sheets, referral cards, job-site signs and simple print advertisements. Coordinate suitable placements through local publications, chambers, trade groups and community opportunities.

Use actual service areas, approved project photos and clear contact details. Print runs, postage, sponsorships and publication charges are paid by the company receiving the advertising.

Help customers find Natural State through AI search

When someone asks an AI search service who to call locally, it may retrieve public websites and business information to build its answer. I will make your public business easy to understand: clear service and county pages, consistent company details, answers to common customer questions, accurate business listings and genuine customer reviews.

At public launch, search indexing, readable page content and business information will be checked so search systems can discover the approved customer pages. These foundations support visibility in conventional search and AI-powered answers. Placement and recommendations depend on each search service.

The current review site remains excluded from search. Proposal pages and private operating records will stay separate from the public marketing site.

Measure inquiries, bookings and completed work

Track advertising spend, inquiry sources, qualified requests, booked visits and completed-job revenue where the source can be linked. Use tagged links and job-source records to calculate cost per qualified inquiry and booked job; reconcile the results with the actual work completed.

Each monthly review will summarize what was spent, which services and locations generated useful inquiries, what changed and where the next budget should go. Campaign adjustments stay within the approved spending limit. Higher budgets require approval.

Included in my service: agreed campaign setup, ad creation, monitoring and monthly review. Paid by Engineering or Siteworks: ad-platform spend, printing, placements and any approved paid marketing tools. New specialist production or a major expansion of campaign scope is agreed separately.
