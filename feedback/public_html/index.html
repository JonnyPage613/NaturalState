FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Service questions

Request a callback for a property-specific question. Include the issue and your preferred callback window.

Request an engineer callback →
Do you do perc tests?

Yes—start with a soil and site evaluation. Arkansas evaluations often use excavated soil pits to assess soil conditions; the evaluator confirms the appropriate method, including percolation testing where applicable. Provide the address or parcel number and who requested the test.

Can you provide a septic “certification” for a home purchase?

That phrase can refer to different inspection, permit or lender requirements. Share the exact request and any existing records. The team will confirm what it can review and whether a separate qualified inspector is needed.

Can a map tell me whether my land supports septic?

Published maps can provide preliminary context. They cannot replace on-site evaluation or professional judgment, and they do not guarantee a permit.

What should I include with my request?

Start with the property address or parcel reference, county, intended use and any deadline. Let the team know whether you have a survey, floor plan or existing permit. Documents can be requested after scope review.

Can I request a visit before closing on the land?

Yes, you can request a review and explain your inspection deadline. Access permission, service scope, fees and availability must be confirmed before any visit is scheduled.

Can I submit several builder lots?

Choose Builder / developer and include the number of properties. Natural State can confirm how to organize the individual project details.

Does submitting a request confirm an appointment?

No. Natural State confirms the scope and availability first. The shared preview uses samples. A real inquiry must still be reviewed and confirmed by the team before any appointment.
